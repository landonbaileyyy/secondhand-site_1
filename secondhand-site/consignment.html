The process

How consigning works

  1. 01

    Bring it in

    Drop off clean, gently-used clothing for men, women, boys, or girls during store hours.

  2. 02

    We review

    Our team checks each piece for condition, style, and season before it's accepted.

  3. 03

    It hits the rack

    Accepted items are tagged, priced, and displayed in the store for shoppers to find.

  4. 04

    You get paid

    When it sells, you earn your share in cash.

Guidelines

What we accept

  • Clean, current-season clothing for men, women, boys, and girls
  • Shoes, handbags, and accessories in good condition
  • Items free of stains, odors, rips, or heavy wear
  • We're unable to accept damaged, stained, or heavily worn items

Not sure if something qualifies? Bring it by or give us a call at (706) 808-0222 — we're happy to take a look.

Common questions

Consignment FAQ

We accept clean, gently-used clothing, shoes, and accessories for men, women, boys, and girls that are current-season and in good condition — free of stains, odors, rips, or heavy wear.

You'll earn a percentage of the sale price once an item sells, paid out in cash. Ask our staff for the current consignment split.

Items are typically displayed for a set consignment period. Unsold pieces after that window may be donated, discounted, or returned, depending on your agreement with us.

Walk-ins are always welcome during store hours. For larger drop-offs, a quick call ahead to (706) 808-0222 helps us prepare.
