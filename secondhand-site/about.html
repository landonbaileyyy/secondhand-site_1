About Second Hand

A neighborhood shop built on good finds and fair prices

Our story

Second Hand Upscale Resale & Consignment opened its doors in the Summerville Square Shopping Center with a simple idea: quality clothing shouldn't come with a premium price tag. We sort through every donation and consignment by hand, so what makes it onto our racks is worth bringing home.

From work clothes and weekend basics to special-occasion pieces and growing-kid wardrobes, we stock something for every member of the family — and our selection changes constantly, so there's always a reason to stop back in.

We're proud to be a small, locally owned business serving Summerville and Chattooga County, and we love helping neighbors both find great clothes and pass along the ones they've outgrown.
